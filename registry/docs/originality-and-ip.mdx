---
title: Originality and IP
description: How AnUIme stays inspired without copying protected characters or franchises.
order: 5
group: Standards
---

Kira, Mochi, Atlas, and all official AnUIme artwork are original creations. The project does not reproduce the names, likenesses, costumes, marks, or signature assets of existing anime franchises.

Contributions must include only assets the contributor owns or has permission to license. References may guide broad genre qualities, but published work must remain independently authored.

Requests to imitate a named copyrighted character or studio style should be reframed as original design attributes such as high contrast, pastel fantasy, technical geometry, or kinetic action.
