An AnUIme recipe chooses approved values for color, shape, structure, motion, density, motion intensity, and display mode.

```ts
type AnuimeRecipeV2 = {
  version: 2;
  colorSystem: "kira" | "mochi" | "atlas";
  shapeSystem: "kira" | "mochi" | "atlas";
  structureSystem: "kira" | "mochi" | "atlas";
  motionSystem: "kira" | "mochi" | "atlas";
  density: "compact" | "comfortable" | "spacious";
  motionLevel: "still" | "calm" | "expressive";
  mode: "light" | "dark" | "system";
};
```

The installable `anuime-recipe` registry item is the canonical implementation used by Component Lab and every installable component. It exports creation, migration, encoding, decoding, validation, and resolution helpers.

Components accept either a complete `recipe` or the backwards-compatible `character` shorthand. When both are present, `recipe` wins.

Version-one five-part and six-part recipe URLs migrate to v2 in memory. Unknown or malformed recipes fail safely and produce a visible recovery message. Recipes contain no generated CSS, executable code, personal information, or secrets.
